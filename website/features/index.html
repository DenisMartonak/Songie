Everything Songies does

A music player for Android, Windows and Linux. The songs on your device and the whole of YouTube Music live in one library, so a playlist can hold both.

One library for everything

  • Your own music, played where it isPoint Songies at your MP3 folders. Files are played in place, never copied.
  • YouTube Music alongside itSongs, albums and artists from YouTube Music sit next to your files, in the same playlists.
  • Playlists that look like yoursReorder by dragging, swipe to edit, and give any playlist your own cover picture.
  • Bring your YouTube playlistsImport a YouTube or YouTube Music playlist by its link. Songies keeps it up to date.
  • One searchSearch your library and YouTube Music together: songs, albums, artists and playlists.

Listening

  • A queue that does what you expectAdd to queue plays songs next, in the order you added them.
  • Shuffle, repeat and radioStart a radio from any song. Media keys, notification and lock-screen controls work too.
  • Now PlayingBig artwork on a blurred cover background, with Now playing, Lyrics and Queue pages you swipe between on phones.
  • Synced lyricsLyrics from LRCLIB and YouTube Music follow the song. Tap a line to jump there.
  • OfflineDownload songs and whole playlists, with an optional Wi-Fi only switch.
  • Your statsTop songs and artists and how long you listened, from the last 7 days up to 12 months.

Listen together

  • Share a link, hear the same songOpen a room and share its link, in Discord or anywhere. Friends who open it hear what you play, at the same moment, each on their own device.
  • You decide who does whatGive each friend a role: Listener, Queue (they can add songs) or Full control.
  • Nothing stops when it endsLeaving a room, or the room ending, never cuts the music. The queue stays and the song carries on.

Make it yours

  • ThemesPick a built-in theme, make your own with your colours and fonts, or let the colours follow the album cover.
  • Your YouTube, if you want itSign in to YouTube for your own recommendations and history. The sign-in stays in your system’s secure storage and is never synced.
  • Discord statusOn Windows and Linux, show what you’re listening to on your Discord profile. Off until you turn it on.

Across your devices

  • Sync, if you want itSign in to a Songies account and your library, playlists, themes, lyrics and stats follow you between devices. Songies works fine signed out.
  • Remote controlRun the music on one device from another one signed in to the same account.
  • Updates inside the appSongies finds a new release, checks it and installs it over the top, keeping your library.
